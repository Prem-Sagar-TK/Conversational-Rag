Acme Retail Customer Refund & Return Policy

1. Standard Return Window
Customers may request a return or full refund within 30 calendar days of product delivery. Products must be in original condition with intact original packaging.

2. Refund Processing Time
Once a returned item is received and inspected at our fulfillment center, refunds are approved within 2 business days. The refund is automatically credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

3. International Orders & Shipping Fees
For international orders outside North America, original shipping fees and customs import duties are non-refundable. International return shipping labels are provided at a flat rate of $15.00 deducted from the refund total.

4. Non-Refundable Items
Gift cards, downloadable software products, and personalized custom items are strictly non-refundable once issued or fulfilled.

5. Damaged or Defective Items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, customers must notify support@acmeretail.com within 7 days of receipt with photo evidence. A replacement or 100% refund including shipping will be processed immediately.
