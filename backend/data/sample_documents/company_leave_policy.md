# Acme Global - Employee Leave & Time-Off Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement
All full-time employees are entitled to **20 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year. Annual leave accrues on a monthly basis starting from the employee's date of hire.

## 2. Leave Carry-Forward Rules
Employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** into the following calendar year. Any carried-forward days must be utilized within the first **90 days (Q1)** of the new year. Any carried-forward leave not taken within this 90-day period will be forfeited without cash compensation.

## 3. Sick Leave & Medical Emergencies
Employees are granted **10 days of paid sick leave** annually. For sick leaves exceeding **3 consecutive days**, an official medical certificate signed by a licensed physician must be submitted to the People Operations portal within 48 hours of return.

## 4. Parental & Caregiver Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**. Secondary caregivers receive **6 weeks of fully paid leave**. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months following the birth or legal adoption of a child.

## 5. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to **5 consecutive business days** of paid bereavement leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).

## 6. Remote Work & Work-from-Anywhere
Employees in good standing are eligible to work remotely from any approved international location for up to **30 calendar days per year**, subject to manager pre-approval and compliance with local tax and visa regulations.
